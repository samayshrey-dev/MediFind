MediAI Privacy Policy

How MediAI collects, uses, protects, and handles your personal data, real-time geolocation, and healthcare commerce information.

Our Privacy Commitment

MediAI is built on principles of healthcare transparency, data minimization, and user sovereignty. We never sell your personal healthcare data or medicine search queries to third-party data brokers or advertisers. All interactions are strictly governed under India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023.

01

Information Collected

MediAI collects only the minimum necessary information required to facilitate medicine discovery, inventory verification, and pharmacy reservation workflows. Information collected is grouped into the following categories:

User-Provided Data

Name, email address, phone number, login credentials, and communication preferences provided during registration.

Automatically Collected Data

Browser user agent, IP address, device telemetry, session duration, and real-time GPS coordinates (when permission is granted).

02

Account Information

When you register an account as a Customer or Pharmacy Merchant, we maintain:

  • Identity & Contact: Your full name, email address, password hash (argon2/bcrypt encrypted), and mobile number for reservation status notifications.
  • Role Designation: Account classification (Customer, Pharmacy Owner, or Administrator) determining access controls and platform permissions.
  • Authentication Records: Login timestamps, active sessions, and cryptographically signed session tokens.
  • Password Security: One-time passwords (OTP) for password recovery are hashed, rate-limited, and automatically expired after 10 minutes.
03

Pharmacy Information

For registered partner pharmacies and chemist retailers, MediAI collects and publicly displays store information to help customers locate medicines:

  • Store Details: Registered pharmacy business name, physical street address, city, state, postal code (PIN code), and customer support phone number.
  • Operating Hours: Daily opening and closing schedules, holiday closures, and live store open/closed status.
  • Live Inventory Catalog: Medicine SKU listings, packaging variants, unit prices, batch numbers, expiry dates, and current stock quantities.
  • Compliance & Verification: State Drug Controller licenses, GSTIN identification, and partner merchant verification status.
04

Location Usage & Geolocation

MediAI uses your device's geographical coordinates (latitude and longitude) strictly to calculate real-world road distances to nearby partner pharmacies:

Explicit Consent & Ephemeral Processing

Geolocation is accessed exclusively when you click "Use My Location" or permit browser geolocation dialogs. We do not track your location in the background when the app is inactive, nor do we construct persistent geographic movement profiles.

05

Search History & AI Queries

When you use MediAI's medicine search bar or the AI Commerce Agent:

  • Query Terms: Searched medicine names, symptoms, or active ingredients are recorded in your personal Search History for your convenience and search suggestions.
  • AI Intent Interpretation: Natural language prompts submitted to the conversational assistant are processed strictly to identify medical intent and match verified catalog items.
  • Audit Trail Logging: AI decision-making steps, constraint validations, and score calculations are recorded in an internal audit log with zero personal identifiers.
  • User Control: You can clear your search history anytime directly from your Search History page.
06

Payment Processing & Security

MediAI supports secure online payments and reservations through certified financial infrastructure:

Secure payments powered by Razorpay

All online transactions (UPI, Credit/Debit Cards, Net Banking, Wallets) are processed directly through Razorpay Software Private Limited, an RBI-authorized Payment Aggregator complying with PCI-DSS Level 1 certification.

  • Zero Card Data Retention: MediAI servers never receive, store, or process raw credit/debit card numbers, CVV codes, or net banking passwords.
  • Transaction Records: We store only the Razorpay Order ID, Payment ID, transaction timestamp, and amount for order fulfillment and customer dispute resolution.
  • Cryptographic Verification: All webhook and payment callback payloads are verified using SHA-256 HMAC cryptographic signatures.

09

Data Retention Policy

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy:

  • Active Accounts: Account profiles and pharmacy inventory catalogs are retained for the duration of the active account relationship.
  • Reservation & Order Records: Completed orders and financial transaction logs are retained for 7 years to comply with statutory accounting and tax regulations under Indian law.
  • Search Queries: Search history entries are stored in your active profile and can be purged on-demand by the user.
  • Security Logs: Server access logs and security telemetry are automatically rotated and purged after 90 days.
10

Deletion Requests & Your Legal Rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, you possess explicit rights regarding your personal information:

Right to Access & Portability

You may request an export of all personal data, reservations, and inventory records associated with your account.

Right to Correction

You can update your personal contact details, pharmacy address, or inventory data anytime via your Profile Settings.

Right to Erasure (Deletion)

You may submit an account and data deletion request by emailing our privacy desk. We will permanently erase non-statutory records within 30 days.

Right to Withdraw Consent

You may revoke location permissions or unsubscribe from restock notifications at any time.
