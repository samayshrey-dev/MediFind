2. Services Offered

MediAI aggregates real-time inventory listings from licensed local pharmacies, allowing users to compare retail prices, view store operating hours, calculate road distance, and place medicine reservations with online payment (powered by Razorpay) or payment on pharmacy pickup.

3. Prescription Drugs Compliance

Schedule H and Schedule X medications under the Indian Drugs and Cosmetics Act strictly require a physical or verified digital prescription upon pickup. Pharmacists reserve the legal right to inspect and verify the prescription before dispensing.
